About

Meet Martin & Julie

Cafe Olivia is a cozy hidden gem at 21 King Street West, run by owners Martin and Julie. Regulars come back for food made fresh daily, very reasonable prices and staff who are always happy to see them, whether it's a quick coffee or a weekly lunch.
